Wireless Battery — build with automatic updates (mytoolkit.ru)
==============================================================

This is a MODIFIED version of Wireless Battery by krisgfx9 and contributors
(https://github.com/krisgfx9/Wireless-Device-Battery-Monitor), licensed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (see LICENSE.txt). It is not an official build.

Modified by Agrysif for mytoolkit.ru, first on 2026-11-19:
- added src/tk_update.cpp: silent automatic updates. Every 6 hours the app downloads
  https://mytoolkit.ru/files/programmy/wireless-battery/latest.json, checks its ECDSA P-256
  signature, downloads the installer it names, checks the installer's SHA-256 and runs it silently.
  Nothing about you or your devices is sent. Only per-user installs update themselves.
- added the tray menu item "Update automatically" (on by default) to turn this off;
- the installer starts the app again after such an update and keeps your "Start with Windows" choice.

Source code of this build: https://github.com/Agrysif/wireless-battery-autoupdate
(every release there also has the complete patched source as wireless-battery-<version>-source.tar.gz).
